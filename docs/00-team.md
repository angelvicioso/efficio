# Team & Roles

_Last updated: 2026-11-25_

| Role | Person | Background | Owns |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Technical lead (CTO)** | Eduardo | Technical | Architecture, build, integrations, data model, security, technical review of data providers |
| **Domain / Operations lead** | Orlando | Logistics operations | What each party (exporter, carrier, trucker, port, warehouse) needs; workflow accuracy; acceptance criteria for stories; industry introductions |
| **Business & Finance lead** | Javier | Financial associate/analyst, commercial real estate (works full time elsewhere) | Business case, market sizing, pricing, unit economics, data-provider cost comparison and deal terms, investor materials, fundraising |

## How the roles work together
- **Data access (biggest risk, R-001):** Orlando finds the contacts → Eduardo checks the technical fit (API, data quality) → Javier compares cost and terms → the team decides.
- **Pilot exporters:** Orlando brings the relationships, and Javier frames the offer (free pilot, pricing later, what we ask in return).
- **Warehouse side (later):** Javier's commercial real estate network may lead to industrial/warehouse owners and operators. See Q-015.

## Working agreements

- Orlando signs off on acceptance criteria before a story moves to **Ready**.
